Work Sample Analyser

Analyse a photo or PDF of student work against your criteria

1 Set your criteria

Paste a rubric, list success criteria (one per line), or start from a preset. The analysis addresses each criterion it finds here.

2 Add the work sample

Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDFs, or short videos — add multiple files if needed. Word documents: save/export as PDF first. Photos are resized in your browser before sending.

🎬 Videos: still frames are sampled in your browser and analysed in sequence, and speech is transcribed on your device (nothing is uploaded for transcription; the speech model downloads once, ~40–80 MB). The analysis identifies key moments against your criteria with timestamps. Best under ~3 minutes; MP4 works most reliably.

Click to choose files or drag & drop here
You can also take a photo or video on a phone or tablet

Downloads a single HTML file containing the work sample, criteria, video frames and transcript, plus a feedback form. Send it to a colleague however you normally share files (email, Teams, OneDrive) — they open it in any browser (no API key needed), write their feedback, and send it back as text. Paste their feedback into “Extra context” to include it in the analysis.

3 Student self-assessment (optional)

Let the student rate their own work against the same criteria first. The analysis will respond to their self-assessment — where their judgement matched, where it differed and why. Great for building feedback literacy.

Analysis

AI-generated analysis — always review against your own professional judgement before using it for assessment or reporting. Avoid uploading work containing sensitive personal information.
