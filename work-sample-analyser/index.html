Work Sample Analyser

Analyse a photo or PDF of student work against your criteria

1 Set your criteria

Paste a rubric, list success criteria (one per line), or start from a preset. The analysis addresses each criterion it finds here.

2 Add the work sample

Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDFs, or short videos — add multiple files if needed. Word documents: save/export as PDF first. Photos are resized in your browser before sending.

🎬 Videos: still frames are sampled from the video in your browser and analysed in sequence — good for performance tasks you can see (PE skills, handwriting process, science practicals, art technique). Audio/speech is not analysed. Best under ~3 minutes; MP4 works most reliably.

Click to choose files or drag & drop here
You can also take a photo or video on a phone or tablet

Analysis

AI-generated analysis — always review against your own professional judgement before using it for assessment or reporting. Avoid uploading work containing sensitive personal information.
